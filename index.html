Marvel Rivals Stats Tracker

An unofficial stats tracker for Marvel Rivals developed by a fellow fan of the game. Feel free to log your stats after each match and review your history. Everything is saved in your browser's local storage. There is also an additional option to export your match data into a CSV file for easier convenience or to help strategize for Esport tournaments.
